A closer look · case 006

Interior lead funnel leak detector

Read full case report
Business analysisSynthetic sample

Question

Which acquisition sources deserve a closer look, and are leads being contacted quickly enough?

Data

192 fictional home-interior enquiries, with a 66-lead follow-up queue. No real customer records were used.

Method

Compared source-level conversion, consultation and won rates, then checked attainment of a four-hour response target.

Finding

Referral leads converted at 41.7% in the sample, against 10.4% for paid social. Four-hour response attainment also varied sharply by source.

Limits

The records are synthetic. The result demonstrates a repeatable analysis workflow; it does not establish real channel performance or causation.

What I would check next

Validate the source definitions, compare lead quality and spend, then test whether faster follow-up changes conversion before recommending budget shifts.

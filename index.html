A closer look

Interior lead-funnel analysis.

One business question, with the data, method, finding, and limits in one place.

Business analysis

Which sources convert—and where does follow-up slip?

Synthetic sample

Data

192 fictional home-interior enquiries and a 66-lead sample follow-up queue. No real customer records were used.

Method

Compared source-level consultation and won rates, then measured attainment of a four-hour response target.

Finding

Referral leads had a 41.7% sample win rate versus 10.4% for paid social. Response-target attainment also varied sharply by source.

Limits

The records are synthetic. This demonstrates an analysis workflow, not real channel performance or evidence that source alone causes conversion.

Next check

Validate source definitions, compare lead quality and spend, and test whether faster follow-up changes conversion before recommending budget shifts.

Data analysis

Employee attrition analysis

Reported

Question

Which groups show higher attrition, and which model catches leavers?

Data

1,470 employee records from a benchmark dataset.

Finding

Attrition was 30.5% for overtime employees versus 10.4% without overtime. Logistic Regression caught more leavers than Random Forest despite lower accuracy.

Limits

This is an observed association, not proof that overtime causes exits. Model performance also depends on class balance and the chosen split.
